I've decided to take more control over my content by adopting **POSSE** - Publish on your Own Site, Syndicate Elsewhere.

## What is POSSE?

Rather than posting directly to Twitter, Bluesky, or LinkedIn, I now:

1. **Write here first** - My site is the canonical source
2. **Syndicate copies** - Share to social platforms with links back
3. **Own my content** - Even if platforms disappear, my posts remain

## Why it matters

Social platforms come and go. Remember Vine? Google+? Twitter's uncertain future? By publishing here first, I'm not at the mercy of any single platform.

## How it works

Each post includes:
- **RSS feed** - Subscribe at [/feed.xml](/feed.xml)
- **Meta tags** - Rich previews when shared
- **Syndication links** - See where else this post lives (below)

Want to implement POSSE on your own site? Check out [IndieWeb.org/POSSE](https://indieweb.org/POSSE) for resources.
